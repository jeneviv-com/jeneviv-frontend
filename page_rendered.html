// How it works

From link to documentation
in under 60 seconds

Create a collection, share a link, let Flutterwave confirm every payment. Your payer list builds itself.

01

Create a collection

Set name, amount, deadline, and member fields. Done in 2 minutes — no IT department, no approval needed.

02

Share the link

Unique payment URL and QR code generated instantly. Drop it in your WhatsApp group — no app download needed.

03

Members pay instantly

Bank transfer via Flutterwave. Webhook confirms in seconds. Receipt email sent. Dashboard updates live.

04

Export your report

One-click PDF — name, ID, amount, date. Ready for your HOD, vendor, auditor, or board.
